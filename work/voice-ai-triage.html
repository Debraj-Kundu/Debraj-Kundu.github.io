ILLUSTRATED SYSTEM CONCEPT
01 / THE PROBLEM

Healthcare front desks spend hours answering and sorting phone calls. The goal was a voice system that could take those calls, work out what each caller needs and send them to the right place.

02 / THE ENGINEERING

Calls arrive through Twilio into LiveKit, where a team of agents handles the conversation: Deepgram for speech-to-text, OpenAI GPT-4.1 for reasoning and ElevenLabs for natural speech.

A dedicated classification agent sorts each call as medical, admin (with subtypes) or unknown so it can be routed correctly.

03 / THE RESULT

The bot serves 1.5M+ patient calls a month, saving hundreds of staff hours. Classification improved accuracy and cut manual triage effort by ~40%.

DETAILS THAT MATTER
  • Real-time: a speech pipeline built for low-latency, natural turn-taking.
  • Multi-agent: conversation and classification separated into focused agents.
  • Scale: runs on the same horizontally scaled platform as the rest of the product.
